How to play

You earn stars from your cities every turn. Spend them on technology, on harvesting resources and building (which grows city population), and on training units. To take a village or enemy city, move a unit onto it, then use Capture on your next turn. When a city's population fills, it levels up and you pick a reward. Ships come from ports and can be upgraded. Make peace through the Diplomacy panel. Domination: be the last tribe standing. Perfection: have the highest score after 30 turns. Desktop: drag to pan and use the wheel to zoom. Mobile: drag, and pinch to zoom.
